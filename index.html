Zapdial 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 8 月 10 日

Zapdial 是一款單手操作輔助工具。它讓你用智慧手錶的旋轉錶圈代替手指， 在手機上完成捲動、返回、回到主畫面等操作，全程不需要碰觸或握持手機。

我們不蒐集任何個人資料

Zapdial 不蒐集、不儲存、不傳送任何個人資料。App 內沒有帳號系統， 沒有分析工具，沒有廣告，也沒有任何第三方追蹤程式庫。 所有運作都在你的手機與手錶之間完成，沒有任何資料離開你的裝置。

關於無障礙服務權限

Zapdial 需要 Android 的無障礙服務權限，才能在你操作手錶時， 於手機目前開啟的 App 上代替你執行一次滑動、點擊或返回。

Zapdial 使用這項權限做的事：

  • 在你操作手錶時，模擬一次對應的觸控動作
  • 讀取目前位於前景的 App 套件名稱，用來決定滑動的位置參數

Zapdial 不會做的事：

  • 不讀取螢幕上的文字、圖片或任何畫面內容
  • 不記錄、不儲存、不傳送你使用了哪些 App
  • 不會在你沒有操作手錶時自行執行任何動作
  • 不將任何資訊傳送到我們或任何第三方的伺服器

在技術上，Zapdial 的無障礙服務宣告為 canRetrieveWindowContent="false"， 也就是它在系統層級就沒有讀取畫面內容的能力。 前景 App 的套件名稱只在裝置的記憶體中即時使用，不會寫入儲存空間，也不會離開裝置。

手錶與手機之間的通訊

手錶端與手機端透過 Google Play 服務的 Wearable Data Layer 通訊， 傳輸內容僅為兩個位元組的指令代碼（例如「往下一則」、「播放或暫停」）。 其中不包含任何個人資訊，也不會經過網際網路。

權限一覽

  • 無障礙服務：模擬觸控操作，用途如上所述
  • 喚醒鎖定（手錶端）：使用控制面時保持螢幕開啟

兒童

Zapdial 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意蒐集兒童的任何資料。

政策變更

本政策如有變更，會更新本頁面並修改上方的更新日期。 若未來版本新增任何會蒐集資料的功能，我們會在 App 內明確告知並取得你的同意。

Zapdial Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Zapdial is a one-handed operation assistance tool. It lets you use your smartwatch's rotating bezel instead of your fingers to scroll, go back, and return to the home screen on your phone, without touching or holding the phone.

We collect no personal data

Zapdial does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking libraries. Everything happens between your phone and your watch. No data leaves your devices.

About the Accessibility Service permission

Zapdial requires Android's Accessibility Service permission in order to perform a single swipe, tap, or back action on the app currently in the foreground, on your behalf, at the moment you operate your watch.

What Zapdial does with this permission:

  • Dispatches one corresponding touch gesture when you operate the watch
  • Reads the package name of the foreground app to choose gesture coordinates

What Zapdial does not do:

  • Does not read text, images, or any on-screen content
  • Does not log, store, or transmit which apps you use
  • Does not perform any action on its own when you are not operating the watch
  • Does not send any information to our servers or any third party

Technically, Zapdial's accessibility service declares canRetrieveWindowContent="false", meaning it has no ability at the system level to read screen content. The foreground package name is used transiently in memory only; it is never written to storage and never leaves the device.

Watch-to-phone communication

The watch and phone apps communicate via the Google Play services Wearable Data Layer. The payload is a two-byte command code (for example "next item" or "play/pause"). It contains no personal information and does not travel over the internet.

Permissions

  • Accessibility Service: to dispatch touch gestures, as described above
  • Wake lock (watch): to keep the screen on while the control surface is open

Children

Zapdial is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If a future version adds any feature that collects data, we will disclose it in the app and ask for your consent.
